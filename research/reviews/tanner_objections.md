# What would a skeptical Professor Tanner object to?

1. **Diffusion-limited kon on a poster.** First-year students will read a bound as a measurement. If T5’s inversion is shown, the word BOUND must be larger than the number.
2. **Closed Wu VoR.** The 1d04 vs glu1 split is abstract-only. An assessor can ask for the truncation table and we cannot produce it from this repo.
3. **Clements is not a glutamate assay.** Using 1.1 mM / 1.2 ms as “the synapse” is a 1992 kinetic inference in culture.
4. **Hu “potential for in vivo” vs 15 min ACV.** The paper’s own protocol time contradicts a neural-dynamics reading of its LOD.
5. **Tanner 2026 tetrahedron is N-protein.** Citing it as glutamate immobilization evidence would be dishonest interface scholarship.
6. **Docking or InstructNA decoration.** Xie/Liu already show docking can invert selectivity; InstructNA is protein SPR with many non-binders. Neither designs a glutamate receptor.
7. **A literature summary without a calculation.** The occupancy inversion is the only piece that is not a recap. If the group cannot explain θ = c/(c+Kd) at 25 nM vs 1.1 mM, the poster fails the course even if the citations are perfect.
8. **Group-final without human approval.** This swarm must not ship a thesis as the group’s thesis.
9. **Scoring-stack inversion.** Isolated Task red-teamers did not reproduce the orchestrator dual-pass ranking (R1 Task: T3 86 sole finalist, T1 last at 79; R2 Task: T5=T4 85, T1 83 not a finalist). Shipping T1 as “the” winner without disclosing that split is process malpractice.
10. **Langmuir 81-fold on a Langmuir–Freundlich EC50.** Hu’s 1.8 nM is a heterogeneous-surface fit. Overlaying a 1:1 81-fold window on that number is a mechanism error unless the exponent is shown.
11. **koff ≈ 1/τ_cleft is the wrong FoM at millimolar Glu.** τ_eq = 1/(kon[Glu]+koff). At 1.1 mM the rising edge can be milliseconds even when t_off is seconds. The sensitivity figure is the honest version of T5.
12. **Hu thesis retina.** If the poster cites “no biological glutamate aptamer data,” an assessor who has the dissertation will end the oral. If the poster cites the retina experiment as real-time neural measurement, the author’s own one-minute / basal-Glu sentence will end it.

Hardest five oral questions (for the winner):

1. Is 15 min the aptamer’s koff, or just when you chose to scan?
2. Why is Hu’s 1.8 nM not “the” Kd of the glutamate aptamer?
3. Can one Langmuir site cover 25 nM and 1.1 mM? Show the 81-fold identity without a review.
4. Why not plot tobramycin IPA 2 ms next to 1.2 ms as proof aptamers are fast enough?
5. What experiment would make you withdraw the thesis?
6. Did Hu’s own retina chapter already do the biological experiment — and what clock did it actually have?
