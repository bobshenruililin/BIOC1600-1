# What would a skeptical Professor Tanner object to?

1. **Diffusion-limited kon on a poster.** First-year students will read a bound as a measurement. If T5’s inversion is shown, the word BOUND must be larger than the number.
2. **Closed Wu VoR.** The 1d04 vs glu1 split is abstract-only. An assessor can ask for the truncation table and we cannot produce it from this repo.
3. **Clements is not a glutamate assay.** Using 1.1 mM / 1.2 ms as “the synapse” is a 1992 kinetic inference in culture.
4. **Hu “potential for in vivo” vs 15 min ACV.** The paper’s own protocol time contradicts a neural-dynamics reading of its LOD.
5. **Tanner 2026 tetrahedron is N-protein.** Citing it as glutamate immobilization evidence would be dishonest interface scholarship.
6. **Docking or InstructNA decoration.** Xie/Liu already show docking can invert selectivity; InstructNA is protein SPR with many non-binders. Neither designs a glutamate receptor.
7. **A literature summary without a calculation.** The occupancy inversion is the only piece that is not a recap. If the group cannot explain θ = c/(c+Kd) at 25 nM vs 1.1 mM, the poster fails the course even if the citations are perfect.
8. **Group-final without human approval.** This swarm must not ship a thesis as the group’s thesis.
