## one-sentence thesis

Present glutamate DNA-aptamer sensors have not jointly demonstrated the molecular recognition, selectivity, kinetics, architecture, and validation needed to measure neurochemical dynamics, because published Kd, apparent Kd, and LOD values attach to non-transferable constructs, glutamate kon/koff are unreported, and a 15 min electrochemical incubation cannot be identified with an inferred 1.2 ms cleft transient (C001, C002, C004, C005, C006, C011, C014, C021, C026, primary-source-supported; C003, hypothesis; C007, C020, unresolved).

## central biochemical mechanism

The receptor is a DNA aptamer, not an enzyme. Reversible occupancy of one immobilized aptamer by glutamate is the quantity that any electrochemical, FET, or SPR readout can at most report:

\[
\text{Apt} + \text{Glu} \underset{k_\text{off}}{\stackrel{k_\text{on}}{\rightleftharpoons}} \text{Apt}\cdot\text{Glu},\qquad
\theta=\frac{[\text{Glu}]}{[\text{Glu}]+K_\text{d}}\ \text{at 1:1 equilibrium},\qquad
K_\text{d}=\frac{k_\text{off}}{k_\text{on}}.
\]

A 1:1 Langmuir isotherm spans only an 81-fold concentration window between 10% and 90% occupancy, so one Kd cannot cover both tonic and cleft glutamate (C013, review-supported; biological set-points from C012 ~25 nM and C011 1.1 mM, both primary-source-supported; C013 notes that those set-points were not measured in the Langmuir source). The tonic-to-cleft gap is ~4.4×10^4-fold (derived from C011 1.1 mM and C012 25 nM). C012 is ambient extracellular glutamate in acute hippocampal slice by tonic NMDAR current, not an extrasynaptic-only assay (C012, primary-source-supported). C011 is an inferred peak and decay tau at cultured hippocampal synapses, not a chemical concentration assay (C011, primary-source-supported).

Time dependence is \(\mathrm{d}\theta/\mathrm{d}t=k_\text{on}[\text{Glu}](1-\theta)-k_\text{off}\theta\). No glutamate aptamer kon/koff were found in the inspected glutamate sensor papers, so Kd cannot be converted into a synaptic response time (C007, unresolved). What is measured instead is protocol wait: Hu 2025 records glutamate ACV after 15 min incubation (C006, primary-source-supported). That measurement_time is ~7.5×10^5-fold longer than the 1.2 ms inferred cleft tau (derived from C006 and C011); this compares incubation to biology, not koff to tau (C007, unresolved; C009, primary-source-supported: a 2 ms IPA clock is interrogation time, not binding koff, and is tobramycin not glutamate).

Recognition numbers are different physical objects on different molecules. Capture-SELEX isolate 1d04 binds glutamate with Kd 12 µM in the indexed abstract (C001, primary-source-supported). Truncated surface-bound glu1 E-AB reports LOD 0.0013 pM by ACV in that same abstract (C002, primary-source-supported). Those two figures are not interchangeable affinity numbers (C003, hypothesis). Hu lists Glu-apt Kd = 12 µM by citing Wu, not by remeasuring the Fc-thiol truncated surface construct (C021, primary-source-supported). On that surface construct, electrochemical apparent Kd is 1.8 nM (Langmuir–Freundlich), distinct from the cited 12 µM solution value (C005, primary-source-supported). Hu Glu-apt is a truncated Fc- and thiol-modified oligo; the authors themselves contrast electrochemical apparent Kd with cited solution-phase values because of 2D confinement (C026, primary-source-supported; confinement is the authors’ interpretation). Hu glutamate MEA LOD is 32 pM in PBS and 51.5 pM in 50% serum (C004, primary-source-supported). Xiao 2025 glutamate SPR Kd is 293 nM while FET practical LOD is 10 fM in 0.1× PBS (C014, primary-source-supported; 0.1× PBS is not 1× aCSF and not in vivo). Abrantes 2025 preprint abstract claims 1 aM glutamate LOD in aCSF on a graphene FET with computationally designed NG-Apt-Glu (C025, primary-source-supported as a preprint claim; in silico design; not a substitute for kon/koff). These rows cannot be averaged into “the glutamate aptamer affinity,” and parent→truncated, solution→surface, buffer→buffer, and architecture→architecture transfers are not licensed (C001–C005, C014, C021, C025, C026).

Immobilization is not a null operation even in non-glutamate systems: E-AB packing density and SAM chemistry change signal gain and apparent Kd for cocaine/thrombin (C018, primary-source-supported; not glutamate). DNA tetrahedron display of Apt48 increased N-protein fiber signal 2–2.5 fold and halved apparent fiber Kd versus Apt48 alone (C015, primary-source-supported; N-protein interface evidence only, not glutamate). Docking scores are not proof of selectivity: predicted aptamer structures and docking scores failed to recover experimental theophylline versus caffeine selectivity (C017, computational illustration). InstructNA generated some stronger SPR binders than top-frequency HT-SELEX clones for LOX1/CXCL5, but most generated sequences were not strong binders (C016, primary-source-supported; protein targets, not glutamate).

Even a genetically encoded glutamate indicator can report a filtered waveform: iGluSnFR fluorescence time courses often last 10–100 times longer than the extracellular lifetime of synaptically released glutamate, in part because the indicator buffers glutamate (C024, primary-source-supported; not an aptamer kinetic). Enzyme glutamate sensors remain subsecond, not millisecond: Rutherford 2007 GlutOx MEA reports 500–800 ms response time in freely moving rats (C022, primary-source-supported); Clay 2018 reports a simulated GlutOx response time of 0.73 s matching experimental 0.8 ± 0.2 s (C023, primary-source-supported; the 0.73 s value is a simulation). No verified in vivo glutamate aptamer sensor is in this ledger; in vivo aptamer FETs were demonstrated for serotonin and dopamine (C020, unresolved as a glutamate negative; scope limit). Park 2023’s table row labeled Glutamate FET is *Plasmodium falciparum* GDH, not neurotransmitter glutamate (C019, primary-source-supported as a mislabel). Amino-acid selectivity (glutamate versus aspartate or other neurochemicals) is not a verified claim in C001–C026 (unresolved).

## three decisive experiments

1. **Same-construct glutamate surface kinetics (proposed experiment).** Perform mass-transport-controlled interrupted-potential amperometry, or SPR/ITC kobs versus [glutamate], on the Hu Fc-thiol truncated Glu-apt gold construct that already has apparent Kd 1.8 nM, LOD 32 pM / 51.5 pM, and a 15 min ACV wait (C004, C005, C006, C026, primary-source-supported). Report kon, koff, and kinetic Kd for that construct only. Tobramycin IPA shows that surface kon/koff can be measured when mass transport is controlled (C008, primary-source-supported: kon 3.5×10^4 M^−1 s^−1, koff 1.39 s^−1, kinetic Kd 41±11 µM; tobramycin parent aptamer, not glutamate). Do not enter C008 or the mixed small-molecule ITC survey (C010, primary-source-supported; not glutamate; abstract only) as glutamate rates. Pass: glutamate koff and equilibration time are reported on the Hu construct and can be compared with inferred 1.2 ms cleft tau (C011, primary-source-supported) and with GlutOx 500–800 ms (C022, primary-source-supported). Fail: kinetics remain protocol waits (C006) or foreign-aptamer substitutions (C007, unresolved; C008, C010 not transferable).

2. **Locked-sequence quantity ledger across architecture and matrix (proposed experiment).** Hold one de-novo-verified glutamate aptamer sequence fixed and measure, as separate columns, solution Kd_molecular (SPR or ITC), surface electrochemical apparent Kd, and sensor_LOD, each in PBS and in 50% serum, without transferring 1d04’s 12 µM onto glu1, Hu Glu-apt, or Xiao FET (C001, C002, C004, C005, C014, C021, primary-source-supported; C003, hypothesis). Include a challenge panel of structurally related amino acids in the same buffer; C001–C026 do not currently supply that selectivity table (unresolved). Do not use Park 2023 as a glutamate FET benchmark (C019, primary-source-supported mislabel). Do not treat Abrantes 1 aM as a SELEX-validated Kd (C025, primary-source-supported preprint claim). Pass: the same construct either collapses or quantitatively explains the 12 µM versus 1.8 nM versus 293 nM versus pM/fM/aM scatter. Fail: architecture or buffer still moves the reported figure by amounts that would be hidden if Kd and LOD were averaged.

3. **Occupancy-window and waveform test against biology and against GlutOx (proposed experiment).** On the same electrode as experiment 1, titrate glutamate through the Herman ambient ~25 nM and Clements inferred 1.1 mM set-points (C012, C011, primary-source-supported) and test whether 10–90% occupancy can cover both, given the 81-fold 1:1 Langmuir window (C013, review-supported). Separately, compare a rapid glutamate step to published GlutOx response times (C022 500–800 ms, primary-source-supported; C023 simulated 0.73 s versus experimental 0.8 ± 0.2 s, primary-source-supported; label any student ODE overlay as a simulation). Interpret any slow fluorescent or electrochemical tail in light of indicator buffering (C024, primary-source-supported; iGluSnFR, not aptamer). Pass: one construct occupies both biological set-points and outruns, or at least matches, GlutOx on a reported response_time. Fail: the device is an incubated LOD assay (C002, C004, C006, C014, C025) rather than a dual-compartment, millisecond glutamate meter.

## one critical limitation

The temporal-mismatch argument is currently strongest as a **gap**, not as a measured glutamate koff. Inspected glutamate aptamer papers do not report kon/koff (C007, unresolved), so 15 min (C006, primary-source-supported, measurement_time) cannot be rewritten as the aptamer’s unbinding rate, and IPA’s 2 ms clock cannot be rewritten as synaptic clearance (C009, primary-source-supported; tobramycin). C011’s 1.1 mM / 1.2 ms figures are a kinetic inference at cultured synapses, not a chemical assay (C011, primary-source-supported). Wu 1d04 versus glu1 details beyond the indexed abstract are unverified because that VoR is closed (C001, C002, primary-source-supported abstracts). Until experiment 1 exists, the defensible statement is that joint kinetic competence is **undemonstrated**, not that glutamate aptamer koff has been shown to be too slow.

## three likely assessor attacks

1. **“15 min is just a convenient wait; the aptamer could still be fast.”** Absorb it: that is exactly C007 (unresolved). C006 (primary-source-supported) is measurement_time, not koff; C009 (primary-source-supported) already forbids equating a millisecond interrogation clock with cleft clearance. The thesis is undemonstrated joint competence, not a measured glutamate koff. Experiment 1 is the reply; importing C008 tobramycin or C010 ITC rates would be an illegal transfer.

2. **“LOD 0.0013 pM / 10 fM / 1 aM proves it can see synaptic glutamate.”** Absorb it: LOD is not Kd, not kon, and not response_time (C002, C004, C014, C025, primary-source-supported; C003, hypothesis). glu1’s 0.0013 pM is a truncated E-AB LOD, not 1d04’s 12 µM Kd (C001, C002). Hu’s 32 pM PBS LOD coexists with a 15 min wait and a 1.8 nM apparent surface Kd (C004, C005, C006). Xiao’s 10 fM FET LOD is in 0.1× PBS beside a 293 nM SPR Kd (C014). Abrantes 1 aM is a preprint in silico FET claim (C025). C013 (review-supported) plus C011/C012 (primary-source-supported) still leave one Langmuir Kd unable to cover tonic nM and cleft mM. Sensitivity without construct-matched kinetics is not dynamics.

3. **“Display, generative design, in vivo aptamer FETs, or review tables already close the gap.”** Absorb it item by item. C015 (primary-source-supported) is DNA-tetrahedron Apt48 on N-protein, not glutamate. C016 (primary-source-supported) is InstructNA on protein targets with many non-binders, not a glutamate SELEX result; GPU training is out of scope. C017 (computational illustration) is a docking failure on theophylline/caffeine, not proof of glutamate selectivity. C020 (unresolved) records in vivo aptamer FETs for serotonin and dopamine, not glutamate. C019 (primary-source-supported) is a GDH mislabel and cannot populate a neurotransmitter glutamate FET table. Enzyme in vivo GlutOx at 500–800 ms (C022, primary-source-supported) is a comparator that is still ~1000-fold slower than 1.2 ms (C011) and is not an aptamer.
