## one-sentence thesis

Glutamate aptamer sensors have not been shown to measure neurochemical dynamics: every published Kd or LOD is construct- and interface-specific (C001, C002, C004, C005, C014, C021, C026), no glutamate aptamer kon/koff exists against inferred 1.2 ms cleft clearance (C007, C011, C006), and a 1:1 Langmuir 10–90% window cannot cover both ~25 nM tonic and 1.1 mM cleft glutamate (C012, C013, C011).

## central biochemical mechanism

The load-bearing event is occupancy of a **surface-tethered** DNA aptamer by glutamate, not a single solution affinity for “the” glutamate aptamer.

In solution, 1:1 occupancy between 10% and 90% spans only 81-fold in concentration (C013). Biology is not inside that window: ambient hippocampal-slice glutamate is about 25 nM (C012), while inferred peak cleft glutamate is 1.1 mM decaying with τ 1.2 ms (C011)—a ~44,000-fold gap (contradictions §3). A single Kd therefore cannot be both a tonic meter and a synaptic meter.

Tethering rewrites that occupancy. Hu’s Glu-apt is a truncated Fc- and thiol-modified oligo; the electrochemical apparent Kd is 1.8 nM (Langmuir–Freundlich), while the 12 µM figure in that paper is a citation of Wu, not a remeasurement of the surface construct, and the authors attribute the discrepancy to 2D confinement (C005, C021, C026). Wu’s own abstract already splits constructs: Capture-SELEX isolate 1d04 Kd 12 µM versus truncated surface-bound glu1 E-AB LOD 0.0013 pM (C001, C002, C003). Xiao reports SPR Kd 293 nM for one glutamate aptamer and a FET LOD of 10 fM in 0.1× PBS for a related device, with response stabilizing at 200 s (C014). Those quantities are not interchangeable and are not transferable across constructs or matrices.

Independent interface experiments show immobilization is not a null operation, but they are **not glutamate data**: E-AB packing density and SAM chemistry change signal gain and apparent Kd (C018, cocaine/thrombin); DNA tetrahedron display of Apt48 increased N-protein fiber signal 2–2.5-fold and halved apparent fiber Kd versus Apt48 alone (C015). They license the mechanistic claim that surface presentation can move gain and apparent Kd; they do not license a glutamate packing-density number.

Kinetics are a separate object from LOD. Inspected glutamate aptamer sensor papers report no kon/koff (C007). Hu’s glutamate ACV uses a 15 min incubation (C006). Xiao’s FET clock is 200 s (C014). IPA can interrogate in 2 ms, but that clock is measurement_time, not binding koff, and the published IPA rates are tobramycin, not glutamate (C008, C009). Enzyme glutamate comparators still sit at 500–800 ms in vivo (C022) or ~0.8 s in a matched model/experiment (C023)—far slower than 1.2 ms cleft τ (C011). Even iGluSnFR time courses often last 10–100 times longer than free synaptic glutamate because the indicator buffers (C024). A pM LOD after minutes does not show that the oligo tracks free glutamate on neurochemical timescales.

## three decisive experiments

**1. Same-construct solution versus surface titration of Hu’s truncated Fc-/thiol-Glu-apt (C026), at more than one packing density.** Measure solution Kd of that oligo and the electrochemical apparent Kd/gain on the same sequence. This tests whether 1.8 nM (C005) is an interface-shifted occupancy parameter relative to the cited 12 µM label (C021), rather than 1d04’s 12 µM (C001) or glu1’s 0.0013 pM LOD (C002). Use C018 only as a packing-density **method analog**; do not transfer cocaine 60–200% gain onto glutamate.

**2. Surface kinetic interrogation (IPA or equivalent) of that same glutamate oligo, with mass transport controlled, reporting kon and koff.** Compare measured koff to Clements τ 1.2 ms (C011) and to Hu’s 15 min wait (C006). C008/C009 show that surface IPA can return kon/koff and that 2 ms is an interrogation clock; do not copy 3.5×10^4 M⁻¹ s⁻¹, 1.39 s⁻¹, or 41 µM onto glutamate (C007).

**3. Occupancy and chemical selectivity of that same immobilized construct in 1× physiological buffer, not 0.1× PBS (C014), across ~25 nM and 1.1 mM glutamate (C012, C011), plus related amino acids.** Ask whether any single surface isotherm can occupy both ends of that range given the 81-fold Langmuir 10–90% span (C013), and whether Hu’s PBS vs 50% serum LOD shift (32 pM vs 51.5 pM, C004) is occupancy or matrix. Selectivity readout is binding/sensor response, not docking scores (C017). Do not treat Abrantes 1 aM preprint FET LOD (C025), Park’s GDH-mislabeled “Glutamate FET” (C019), or serotonin/dopamine in vivo aptamer FETs (C020) as glutamate-dynamics evidence.

## one critical limitation

Wu’s VoR is closed, so 1d04 versus glu1 beyond the indexed abstract is unverified (C001–C003; contradictions limitations). Clements 1.1 mM / 1.2 ms is a kinetic inference at cultured synapses, not a chemical assay (C011). Herman ~25 nM is slice ambient glutamate with intact transport, not an extrasynaptic-only free pool (C012). Xiao’s 10 fM LOD is in 0.1× PBS, not 1× aCSF (C014). No glutamate aptamer kon/koff is in the ledger (C007), and no verified in vivo glutamate aptamer sensor is in the ledger (C020). The thesis can show that current Kd/LOD/incubation claims do not license neurochemical dynamics; it cannot state the true surface rates of Glu-apt.

## one computational contribution

No InstructNA training (C016 is LOX1/CXCL5 protein SPR; most generated sequences were not strong binders) and no docking-as-proof (C017: docking failed to recover theophylline vs caffeine selectivity). CPU-feasible, per-construct arithmetic only: (i) plot separate 1:1 Langmuir occupancy curves using each construct’s **own** reported number—1d04 12 µM (C001), Hu electrochemical apparent Kd 1.8 nM (C005), Xiao SPR 293 nM (C014)—never averaged, never treating LOD as Kd (C002, C004, C014, C025); overlay the 81-fold 10–90% bar (C013) on 25 nM and 1.1 mM (C012, C011); (ii) compute the koff implied by tracking τ = 1.2 ms as ~1/τ (C011) and place that requirement on a clock axis with the measurement times that actually exist (15 min, C006; 200 s, C014; GlutOx 500–800 ms, C022; 0.73 s simulated / 0.8 ± 0.2 s experimental, C023). Ding ITC kon/koff ranges (C010) and tobramycin IPA rates (C008) may appear only as labeled non-glutamate bounds, not as Glu-apt parameters. This checks consistency of published numbers; it does not design a binder.

## three likely assessor attacks

**1. “The aptamer’s affinity is 12 µM / 1.8 nM / 0.0013 pM — pick one and the sensor covers glutamate.”** Those are different quantities on different constructs (C001 vs C002 vs C005 vs C014). Hu’s 12 µM is a citation, not a remeasurement of the Fc-thiol surface oligo (C021, C026). Kd, electrochemical apparent Kd, and LOD are not interchangeable (C003; contradictions §1). Averaging them is construct/matrix transfer.

**2. “pM LOD plus a 2 ms IPA clock means it can follow 1.2 ms synapses.”** Hu’s glutamate assay incubates 15 min (C006); Xiao’s FET stabilizes at 200 s (C014); no glutamate aptamer kon/koff was found (C007). IPA’s 2 ms is measurement_time on a tobramycin E-AB, not glutamate koff (C009, C008). GlutOx still responds in 500–800 ms or ~0.8 s (C022, C023). iGluSnFR already reports a waveform 10–100× longer than free synaptic glutamate (C024). LOD does not license an unfiltered neurochemical time course.

**3. “Dock or generate a tighter aptamer, then implant it like dopamine/serotonin aptamer FETs.”** Docking scores failed to recover experimental theophylline vs caffeine selectivity (C017). InstructNA produced some stronger protein SPR binders than top HT-SELEX clones but most generated sequences were not strong binders, and it was not a glutamate aptamer (C016). Abrantes NG-Apt-Glu is an in silico preprint LOD claim, not a SELEX-validated glutamate Kd in the inspected abstract (C025). There is no verified in vivo glutamate aptamer sensor in this ledger (C020). Park’s “Glutamate FET” row is *P. falciparum* GDH, not neurotransmitter glutamate (C019).
