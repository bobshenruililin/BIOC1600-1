## one-sentence thesis

A glutamate aptamer sensor cannot currently be claimed to measure neurochemical dynamics, because no named construct jointly supplies a transferable recognition constant, glutamate `kon`/`koff`, an occupancy window that covers tonic-to-cleft concentrations, an architecture-matched isotherm, and in vivo validation: 1d04 `Kd` 12 µM [C001], glu1 LOD 0.0013 pM [C002], Hu cited 12 µM [C021], Hu apparent `Kd` 1.8 nM [C005], Hu LOD 32 pM [C004], and Xiao SPR 293 nM / FET 10 fM [C014] are different quantity types on different constructs [C003, C026], glutamate rates are unreported [C007], and 15 min ACV [C006] or 200 s FET stabilization [C014] is not the inferred 1.2 ms cleft transient [C011].

## central biochemical mechanism

Glutamate is a small ligand; a DNA aptamer reports it only by occupancy of a fold that, once tethered, is transduced (E-AB/ACV or FET). Occupancy of a 1:1 site is Langmuir: \(\theta = c/(c+K_\mathrm{d})\). Between 10% and 90% occupancy that isotherm spans only 81-fold in concentration [C013, review-supported], while hippocampal biology is inferred peak cleft 1.1 mM decaying with \(\tau\) 1.2 ms [C011] versus ambient slice glutamate ~25 nM [C012] — a ~44,000-fold gap [contradictions.md §3], so one \(K_\mathrm{d}\) cannot be both a tonic-nM meter and a cleft-mM meter [C013 + C011 + C012]. Surface constructs are not the solution isolate: Hu’s Glu-apt is a truncated Fc- and thiol-modified oligo whose electrochemical apparent \(K_\mathrm{d}\) is 1.8 nM (Langmuir–Freundlich) after the authors themselves contrast 2D confinement with a cited 12 µM solution label [C005, C021, C026]; Wu’s 12 µM is Capture-SELEX isolate 1d04, not truncated glu1, and glu1’s 0.0013 pM is a surface E-AB LOD, not a \(K_\mathrm{d}\) [C001, C002, C003]. Xiao’s SPR \(K_\mathrm{d}\) 293 nM and FET LOD 10 fM (0.1× PBS) are likewise two quantity types, not one affinity [C014]. Kinetics of occupancy require glutamate `kon` and `koff`; none were found in the inspected glutamate sensor papers [C007]. Hu’s protocol waits 15 min before ACV [C006]; Xiao’s FET response is described as stabilizing at 200 s [C014]; those are `measurement_time`, not `koff` (the 2 ms IPA clock on a tobramycin E-AB is also `measurement_time`, not a glutamate rate [C009, C008 — not transferable]). Even a protein glutamate indicator (iGluSnFR) can report a waveform 10–100 times longer than free extracellular glutamate because the sensor buffers ligand [C024 — not an aptamer kinetic]. Amino-acid selectivity of these glutamate aptamers is not a verified number in C001–C026; Hu’s LOD shift 32 pM (PBS) vs 51.5 pM (50% serum) is a matrix effect, not a competitor \(K_\mathrm{d}\) [C004].

## three decisive experiments

1. **Construct-matched quantity-type panel (same oligo, same buffer, four readouts).** On one named glutamate sequence, measure solution-phase affinity if accessible, SPR \(K_\mathrm{d}\), electrochemical apparent \(K_\mathrm{d}\)/Langmuir–Freundlich EC50, and LOD. Score success only if the four numbers are labeled as distinct quantity types and compared with the existing split: 1d04 12 µM [C001] vs glu1 LOD 0.0013 pM [C002] vs Hu citation 12 µM [C021] vs Hu 1.8 nM [C005] vs Hu 32 / 51.5 pM [C004] vs Xiao 293 nM vs 10 fM [C014]. Do not average. This is the recognition test [C003, C026]. Amino-acid competitors belong on this same chip; C001–C026 contain no glutamate-vs-aspartate selectivity ratio, so selectivity is currently `unresolved`, not a transferred property.

2. **Glutamate-construct IPA `kon`/`koff` (method borrowed; numbers not transferred).** Interrogate Hu-style Glu-apt and/or the Xiao SPR construct with IPA under mass-transport control, as demonstrated for a tobramycin parent aptamer (`kon` 3.5×10⁴ M⁻¹ s⁻¹, `koff` 1.39 s⁻¹, kinetic \(K_\mathrm{d}\) 41±11 µM [C008] — **tobramycin, not glutamate**). Report glutamate `kon`, `koff`, and kinetic \(K_\mathrm{d}\) on the named surface construct [C007 is the gap this closes]. Compare those rates to inferred cleft \(\tau\) 1.2 ms [C011] and to existing assay clocks (15 min [C006]; 200 s [C014]). The 2 ms IPA interrogation interval is a measurement clock, not synaptic clearance [C009]. Do not fill a null glutamate rate with Ding’s non-glutamate ITC `kon`/`koff` survey [C010], GlutOx 500–800 ms in rats [C022], Clay’s 0.73 s simulated / 0.8±0.2 s experimental GlutOx response [C023], or iGluSnFR 10–100-fold filtering [C024].

3. **Two-point occupancy and packing-density challenge on the glutamate E-AB.** Calibrate the same surface construct near 25 nM and near 1.1 mM (or the nearest accessible pair) and test whether one 1:1 Langmuir 81-fold 10–90% window [C013] can cover both biological concentrations [C012, C011]. Independently titrate packing density / SAM chemistry on that glutamate oligo because immobilization is not a null operation on other E-ABs (cocaine/thrombin signal gain 60–200% and apparent \(K_\mathrm{d}\) shift [C018 — **not glutamate numbers**]). DNA-tetrahedron display that raised N-protein fiber signal 2–2.5-fold [C015] is N-protein only and must not be cited as a glutamate gain. Park 2023’s “Glutamate FET” row is *P. falciparum* GDH, not neurotransmitter glutamate, and is excluded from comparator tables [C019].

## one critical limitation

Glutamate aptamer `kon`/`koff` are absent from the inspected sensor papers [C007, unresolved], so LOD (0.0013 pM [C002]; 32 pM [C004]; 10 fM in 0.1× PBS [C014]; preprint 1 aM [C025]) cannot be converted into a claim that the sensor follows 1.2 ms cleft dynamics [C011]. There is also no verified in vivo glutamate aptamer sensor in this ledger [C020]; serotonin/dopamine aptamer FETs do not transfer. Clements 1.1 mM / 1.2 ms is a kinetic inference, not a chemical assay [C011]; Herman ~25 nM is slice ambient glutamate, not extrasynaptic-only [C012]; Wu VoR is closed beyond the indexed abstract [C001, C002]; Xiao’s FET buffer is 0.1× PBS, not 1× aCSF [C014].

## one computational contribution

CPU-only occupancy-and-clock ledger (spreadsheet or a few dozen lines of Python), **not docking**. For each quantity that is actually labeled \(K_\mathrm{d}\) or electrochemical EC50 — 1d04 12 µM [C001], Hu 1.8 nM [C005], Xiao SPR 293 nM [C014] — compute Langmuir \(\theta\) at 25 nM and 1.1 mM and the 81-fold 10–90% concentration window [C013], with the quantity type printed on every point. Plot LOD values (glu1 0.0013 pM [C002]; Hu 32 / 51.5 pM [C004]; Xiao FET 10 fM [C014]; Abrantes 1 aM preprint [C025]) on a **separate** axis as `sensor_LOD`, never as \(K_\mathrm{d}\) [C003]. Overlay assay clocks (15 min [C006]; 200 s [C014]; IPA 2 ms interrogation [C009]) against 1.2 ms [C011] and enzyme comparators (500–800 ms [C022]; 0.73 / 0.8±0.2 s [C023]) as `measurement_time`/`response_time`, not as aptamer `koff`. Docking scores failed to recover experimental theophylline vs caffeine selectivity [C017]; InstructNA SPR hits are LOX1/CXCL5 protein binders, not glutamate [C016]; both stay out of the glutamate occupancy plot.

## three likely assessor attacks

1. **“Hu cites 12 µM, so the sensor’s affinity is 12 µM (or 1.8 nM, or 32 pM, pick one).”** Collapse of C001/C021 (`Kd_molecular` citation) with C005 (surface EC50) and C004/C002/C014 (`sensor_LOD`). Rebuttal: Wu 12 µM is isolate 1d04, not glu1 [C001, C002, C003]; Hu did not remeasure 12 µM on the Fc-thiol truncated oligo [C021, C026]; 1.8 nM is electrochemical apparent \(K_\mathrm{d}\) [C005]; 32 pM is LOD in PBS [C004].

2. **“fM–aM LOD means it can read 25 nM tonic glutamate in real time.”** LOD is not occupancy, not `kon`/`koff`, and not in vivo. Xiao 10 fM is in 0.1× PBS with ~200 s stabilization [C014]; Abrantes 1 aM is a preprint graphene FET on a computationally designed oligo [C025]; Hu still incubates 15 min [C006]; glutamate rates remain unreported [C007]; iGluSnFR already shows that a glutamate-binding reporter can output a filtered waveform [C024].

3. **“Use tobramycin IPA rates, cocaine packing gains, InstructNA, tetrahedron display, Park’s glutamate FET, or serotonin in vivo as glutamate proof.”** Each is a forbidden property transfer: C008/C009 tobramycin; C018 cocaine/thrombin; C016 protein SELEX/InstructNA; C015 N-protein; C019 PfGDH; C020 serotonin/dopamine FETs. Method may be reused on a named glutamate construct; the numbers may not. Docking as a substitute for experiment is independently blocked by C017.
