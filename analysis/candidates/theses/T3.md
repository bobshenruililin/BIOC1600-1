## one-sentence thesis
Tagged evidence does not show that any glutamate aptamer construct can measure neurochemical dynamics: a 1:1 Langmuir isotherm spans only 81-fold from 10% to 90% occupancy (C013), ambient hippocampal-slice glutamate is about 25 nM (C012) while inferred cleft peak is 1.1 mM decaying with τ 1.2 ms (C011)—a ~44,000-fold gap—and inspected glutamate aptamer papers report no kon/koff (C007).

## central biochemical mechanism
For 1:1 Langmuir binding, occupancy is θ = [Glu] / ([Glu] + Kd), so the 10–90% window is fixed at 81-fold in concentration and cannot be stretched to cover both 25 nM tonic and 1.1 mM cleft glutamate with one Kd (C013, C012, C011). That occupancy argument is construct-locked: Capture-SELEX isolate 1d04 has Kd 12 µM in the indexed abstract (C001); truncated surface-bound glu1 E-AB reports LOD 0.0013 pM (C002); those are different objects, not interchangeable affinity numbers (C003). Hu’s surface Glu-apt electrochemical apparent Kd is 1.8 nM (Langmuir–Freundlich in PBS ACV) and is distinct from the 12 µM solution value Hu cites from Wu rather than remeasures on the Fc-thiol truncated oligo (C005, C021, C026); Hu’s LODs are 32 pM in PBS and 51.5 pM in 50% serum (C004), not Wu’s 0.0013 pM. Xiao’s SPR Kd is 293 nM on one glutamate aptamer while the FET practical LOD is 10 fM in 0.1× PBS on the same family (C014)—buffer and transduction are not 1× aCSF. Dynamics additionally require that θ(t) track [Glu](t): Clements’ inferred cleft transient decays with τ 1.2 ms (C011), but Hu incubates 15 min before ACV (C006), Xiao’s FET response is noted to stabilize at 200 s (C014), and no glutamate aptamer kon/koff appear in the inspected papers (C007). Hu’s 0.1 nM–10 µM semi-log span is an analytical calibration window, not proof that one surface isotherm occupies both biological compartments.

## three decisive experiments
1. **Construct-locked occupancy at both biological poles.** On one named glutamate sensor (do not mix 1d04, glu1, Hu Glu-apt, Xiao SPR/FET, or Abrantes NG-Apt-Glu), measure the binding isotherm in the matrix used for the claim (PBS vs 50% serum already split in C004; not 0.1× PBS from C014). Compute occupancy at 25 nM and at 1.1 mM from that isotherm only (C012, C011, C013). Success is dual-pole occupancy inside a defined reporting band on that construct; failure is a single Kd/LOD/EC50 asked to stand in for both compartments.
2. **Glutamate-aptamer kon/koff on the same surface construct.** Measure association and dissociation for the glutamate oligo itself. Surface IPA can report kon/koff when mass transport is controlled, but the available numbers are tobramycin (kon 3.5×10^4 M^−1 s^−1, koff 1.39 s^−1, Kd,kinetic 41±11 µM) and must not be transferred (C008). IPA’s 2 ms clock is measurement_time, not koff and not synaptic 1.2 ms clearance (C009, C011). Ding’s ITC survey (kon 2×10^5 to 96 M^−1 s^−1; koff 1.03×10^−3 to 0.012 s^−1 over Kd 28 nM to 864 µM) is mixed DNA aptamers, not glutamate (C010). Compare the measured glutamate koff and equilibration time to τ 1.2 ms (C011) and to Hu’s 15 min ACV wait (C006).
3. **Waveform vs free glutamate, with enzyme and protein sensors as comparators not proxies.** Deliver a known [Glu](t) and test whether the aptamer report matches free glutamate. iGluSnFR time courses often last 10–100 times longer than the extracellular lifetime of synaptically released glutamate, in part because the indicator buffers (C024)—so a filtered protein-sensor waveform does not license an unlabeled aptamer-neurotransmission claim from LOD. GlutOx MEA response is 500–800 ms in freely moving rats (C022); Clay’s enzyme-electrode model gives a simulated 0.73 s matching experimental 0.8 ± 0.2 s (C023), still far slower than 1.2 ms (C011). There is no verified in vivo glutamate aptamer sensor in this ledger (C020).

## one critical limitation
No glutamate aptamer kon/koff were found in inspected glutamate sensor papers (C007), so the “dynamics” half of the test cannot be closed empirically without illegal transfer of tobramycin IPA (C008) or mixed-aptamer ITC (C010); Clements’ 1.1 mM / 1.2 ms pole is itself a kinetic inference at cultured hippocampal synapses, not a chemical assay (C011), and Herman’s 25 nM is slice ambient glutamate by tonic NMDAR current, not an extrasynaptic-only measurement (C012).

## one computational contribution
CPU-only **construct atlas + Langmuir occupancy**, not docking. Tabulate each construct’s own quantity and refuse averages: 1d04 Kd 12 µM (C001); glu1 E-AB LOD 0.0013 pM (C002); Hu cited 12 µM label vs 1.8 nM electrochemical apparent Kd vs 32 / 51.5 pM LODs (C021, C005, C004); Xiao SPR Kd 293 nM vs FET LOD 10 fM in 0.1× PBS (C014); Abrantes preprint FET LOD 1 aM in aCSF for in silico NG-Apt-Glu (C025). For each Kd or apparent Kd separately, plot θ([Glu]) and mark 25 nM, 1.1 mM, and the 81-fold 10–90% band (C012, C011, C013); treat Hu 0.1 nM–10 µM as a calibration axis only. Optionally scan a kinetic ODE dθ/dt = kon[Glu](1−θ) − koff θ driven by an exponential 1.1 mM, τ 1.2 ms input (C011), labeled **simulation**, with kon/koff as free parameters—not C008 or C010 assigned to glutamate—to show which rate pairs could follow the cleft transient versus a 15 min wait (C006). Docking scores are excluded as proof: predicted structures and docking failed to recover experimental theophylline vs caffeine selectivity (C017). InstructNA’s stronger SPR binders are LOX1/CXCL5 protein targets, and most generated sequences were not strong binders (C016). Clay 0.73 s is a GlutOx electrode **simulation**, not an aptamer result (C023).

## three likely assessor attacks
1. **“Hu already calibrates 0.1 nM–10 µM, so tonic and a wide dynamic range are covered.”** That span is a semi-log analytical window, not occupancy at 25 nM and 1.1 mM on a 1:1 Langmuir (C013, C012, C011); 10 µM is not the 1.1 mM cleft pole; Hu’s 1.8 nM figure is a surface Langmuir–Freundlich apparent Kd under 2D confinement, not a dual-compartment proof (C005, C026); the assay waits 15 min (C006).
2. **“Wu’s 0.0013 pM LOD (or Xiao’s 10 fM / Abrantes’ 1 aM) proves the aptamer is sensitive enough for 25 nM.”** LOD is not Kd and not occupancy (C003): 0.0013 pM is truncated glu1 E-AB, not 1d04’s 12 µM (C002, C001); Xiao’s 10 fM is CNT FET in 0.1× PBS beside SPR Kd 293 nM (C014); Abrantes 1 aM is a preprint graphene FET with computationally designed NG-Apt-Glu (C025). None of those LOD rows license millisecond cleft tracking (C007, C011).
3. **“Aptamers are fast: IPA is 2 ms, tobramycin koff is 1.39 s^−1, and GlutOx already works in vivo.”** The 2 ms figure is interrogation time, not binding koff or 1.2 ms cleft clearance (C009, C011); tobramycin IPA rates are not glutamate (C008); mixed-aptamer ITC rates are not glutamate (C010); GlutOx is an enzyme comparator at 500–800 ms in rats and simulated 0.73 s, not an aptamer, and still far slower than 1.2 ms (C022, C023, C011); in vivo aptamer FETs here are serotonin and dopamine, not glutamate (C020). Park’s glutamate FET row is Plasmodium GDH, not neurotransmitter glutamate (C019).
