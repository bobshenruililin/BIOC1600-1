## 1. Two clocks

- **Claim.** A 15 min glutamate ACV wait is not a 1.2 ms cleft transient; a 2 ms IPA clock is interrogation, not glutamate koff.
- **IDs.** C006, C011, C009, C007
- **Figure.** Log-time ladder in `analysis/accepted/occupancy_kinetics/figures/clocks.svg` (BOUND vs MEASURED vs INFERENCE).

## 2. Construct × quantity atlas

- **IDs.** C001, C002, C004, C005, C014, C021

## 3. Occupancy inversion (computational)

- **IDs.** C001, C005, C012, C011, C027
- **Figure.** `analysis/accepted/occupancy_kinetics/figures/occupancy.svg`
